Where Every Event

"Every event is more than a gathering — it is an experience, a memory, and a reflection of the people behind it."

Radiance Events was founded by two passionate professionals who recognized a clear need in Ethiopia: world-class event experiences delivered with precision, elegance, and deeply personal care.

Built on the belief that every client deserves dedicated attention and flawless execution, Radiance Events is your trusted partner for every occasion that matters.
